# Food & Beverage Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes Food & Beverage sales data to provide insights into revenue performance, sales channels, product categories, and customer purchasing trends.  
The dashboard highlights key business metrics to support sales optimization and strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze revenue and order trends over time
- Monitor sales performance across different channels
- Identify top-performing product categories and product groups
- Evaluate salesperson performance based on revenue and orders
- Support business decisions with data-driven sales insights

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Source:** Food & Beverage Sales dataset
- **Scope:** Sales performance and business analytics
- **Includes:** Revenue, orders, product categories, sales channels, salespersons, and ticket price data

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** $17.91M
- **Average Ticket Price (ATP):** $340.7

---

  ## 📈 Insights

- The largest share of revenue came from Food products, contributing **91.39%** compared to only **8.61%** for Drinks, showing that Food products are the main driver of sales.

- Food products represented the highest share of orders with **90.21%**, while Drinks accounted for **9.79%**, reflecting stronger customer demand for Food products.

- Sales and orders peaked in **October** with **6,105 orders**, while the lowest performance was recorded in **March** with **2,719 orders**, indicating stronger demand during Q4.

---

## 📌 Impacts

- The business shows high dependency on Food products compared to Drink products.  
- Customers show a stronger preference for Food products.  

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Increase focus on promoting Drink products.  
- Customer service teams should highlight promotions and encourage customers to try other product categories.  
- Organize events and promotional campaigns during periods of low revenue and order volume to increase customer interest and sales.
