# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes HR data to provide insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and departmental performance.  
The dashboard highlights key HR metrics to support workforce planning and employee retention strategies.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition across departments  
- Monitor active employees and workforce demographics  
- Identify trends in employee satisfaction and performance  
- Compare income distribution by job role  
- Support HR decision-making with data-driven insights  

## 📂 Dataset
- **Source:** HR Analytics dataset  
- **Rows:** 1,470 employee records  
- **Columns:** HR-related fields including age, department, education, job role, income, and attrition status  
- **Scope:** Employee workforce analysis  

## 📊 Key Metrics
- **Total Employees:** 1.47K  
- **Active Employees:** 1.233K  
- **Attrition Count:** 237  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%  
- **Average Employee Age:** 37  

## 📈 Insights
- The largest share of active employees comes from the **R&D department**, making it the company’s biggest workforce segment.  
- Employees with a **Bachelor’s degree** represent the highest percentage of the workforce, while employees with doctoral degrees represent the lowest share.  
- The majority of employees fall within the **25–44 age group**, indicating a workforce dominated by experienced mid-career professionals.  
- **Sales Representatives** have one of the lowest average monthly incomes compared to several other job roles.  
- Job satisfaction levels vary significantly across job roles, with some technical and sales-related positions showing lower satisfaction scores.  


## 📌 Business Impact
- High dependency on employees with bachelor’s degrees supports operational needs but may limit advanced technical expertise in the long term.  
- Lower salaries in some job roles may contribute to higher attrition rates and reduced employee retention.  
- A balanced workforce age structure improves organizational stability and productivity.  


## 💡 Recommendations
- Increase focus on hiring employees with advanced educational qualifications for specialized roles.  
- Review salary structures for lower-paid job roles to improve employee retention.  
- Continue maintaining a balanced age distribution across the workforce.  
- Develop employee engagement and satisfaction initiatives in departments with lower satisfaction scores.
